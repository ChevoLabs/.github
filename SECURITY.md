# Security policy

Thank you for helping keep ChevoLabs and the people who use our software safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report privately — **do not** open a public issue, discussion or pull request about a vulnerability.

1. **A website or service we run:** use the contact in its `/.well-known/security.txt` if it publishes one.
2. **A public repository in this organization:** use **Security → Report a vulnerability** on that repository
   (GitHub private vulnerability reporting).
3. **Anything else:** email **chevolabs@gmail.com** with the subject line **`Security report`**.

Please include what you found and where (URL, request or repository file), steps to reproduce, and the impact you
expect. Do not include other people's personal data in your report.

We aim to acknowledge reports within **5 business days** and to keep you updated until the issue is resolved.

## Out of scope

Denial-of-service, spam, social engineering, physical attacks, issues in third-party platforms we use (report those to
the platform), and findings from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact.

## Good-faith research

If you act in good faith — avoid privacy violations and service disruption, only test against accounts and data you
own, and give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosure — we will not pursue action against you for your
research.
